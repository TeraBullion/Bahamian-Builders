Building strong foundations and lasting relationships across the United States since 2010.

Our Story

Bahamian Roots, Texas Grit

Founded in 2010 in Houston, Texas by a Bahamian-born entrepreneur, Bahamian Builders LLC was built on island work ethic and Texas ambition. The name reflects our founder's Bahamian heritage — a reminder of where we come from and the values we carry: resilience, integrity, and pride in every job. What started as a small, dedicated crew has grown into one of the most respected site work and concrete companies in the Greater Houston area and beyond.

Over the past fifteen years, we've completed more than 250 projects across the Greater Houston area and beyond. From residential foundations and driveways to large-scale commercial site preparation, our commitment to quality craftsmanship, honest pricing, and on-time delivery has earned us the trust of homeowners, developers, and businesses alike.

Our Values

What Drives Us

Our core values guide every project we take on and every relationship we build. They're the foundation of everything we do.

Quality First

We never cut corners. Every pour, every grade, and every cut meets our exacting standards. Our work is built to last through sun, salt, and storms — because that's what the United States demands.

Texas Integrity

Honesty and transparency are at the heart of how we operate. We provide clear estimates, communicate openly throughout every project, and stand behind our work with confidence.

Community Built

We're proud to hire local talent and invest in our Bahamian communities. When we build a foundation, we're not just building structures — we're building futures for our neighbors and families.
